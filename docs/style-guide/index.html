Visual style guide

One look for the website, slides and print: all five Nordic countries given equal weight, plain system type, and the club logo kept clear.

Logo

The club seal

Use the seal as supplied. It carries the five Nordic flags, so it needs no extra decoration. The circular version is the default; the shield-only version is for tight spaces.

On light. Primary use on white or the light gray page background.

On dark. Works on navy and on any Nordic color. Keep the seal's own colors.

Shield only. For favicons and spaces under 48 px where the ring text would be unreadable.

Clear space and size

¼ width
  • Keep a quarter of the logo's width free on all sides.
  • Minimum size: 48 px on screen, 15 mm in print.
  • Corner placement on slides: 0.45 in at the footer, 1.1 in on covers.

Do and don't

✓Place on solid color or a darkened photo.
✓Scale proportionally.
✕Recolor, outline, rotate or stretch the seal.
✕Put it on a busy, un-darkened photo.
✕Add shadows, glows or effects.

How to use it

Rotate, don't pick a favorite

Across a deck or a page, give each country a turn. On slides, use the Title, Section and Statement versions for all five.

Solid fields, white or ink text

Use a country color as a full background with white text. Sweden yellow takes dark navy text instead.

Neutrals carry the rest

Page background light gray, cards white, text ink. Color is for emphasis, not for every surface.

Rules

  • Headings use a token; don't set weight, tracking or leading by hand.
  • One page title (h1) per page.
  • Uppercase only for the eyebrow tier, at small sizes (12–14 px).
  • Never uppercase headings or body text.
  • No extra-heavy weights (black/extrabold); use bold 700.
  • Left-align paragraphs; center only titles.

Icons

Use Heroicons (outline) through the app's <.icon name="hero-…"> component. In slides, place a white outline icon on a solid Nordic-color circle; the PowerPoint template has an icon library ready to copy.

Imagery

Real people, real gatherings

Use candid photos of members: bonfires, dinners, the cabins. Avoid stock imagery. Warm light and group shots fit best.
